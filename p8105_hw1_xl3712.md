Numeric and logical vectors can be used to calculate the mean, while
character and factor vectors cannot.

``` r
as.numeric(df$vec_logical)
as.numeric(df$vec_char)
as.numeric(df$vec_factor)
```

Logical and factor vectors can be converted to numeric vectors, while
character vectors cannot. R treats `TRUE` as 1 and `FALSE` as 0, which
explains why the mean of the logical vector can be calculated. A factor
can be converted to numeric values, but `as.numeric()` returns its
internal level codes rather than meaningful numeric values.
